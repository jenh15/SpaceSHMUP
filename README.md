# SpaceSHMUP

## Objective
<p> This is a single player survival game. The player has three lives. A life is lost everytime an enemy(spaceship) comes in contact with the player's ship. The arrow keys work to move the ship as desired, and the shift bar launches projectiles that damage the opposing ships. The ships are destroyed once their health is depleted.  </p>

## Enhancements
<p>Initially, the game did not have a life count or a high score tracker. Adding these components gave it a more competetetitive nature. The highscore tracks how many points you earn based on the difficulty of the ships you destroy. The life count adds a sense of urgency. These allow the player to compete with themselves!</p>
